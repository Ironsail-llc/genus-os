Memory search now recalls what was actually said, not only the facts extracted from it: after each saved chat exchange the engine splits the session into topical segments in the background, and `search_memory` returns the matching ones beside facts, each naming its conversation and turn range. A private conversation is recalled only for its owner (the member whose chat it is, or the operator for the main session) while they are talking to the agent in that conversation, whatever the data-scoping setting — never by a scheduled or workspace run acting as them — a workspace conversation reaches only its members, and a conversation with no known owner is recalled by no one; long messages are split, never cut. Segments follow the chat they copy: a deleted session takes its segments with it, and the nightly chat TTL prunes them after 90 days. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#conversation-segments).
