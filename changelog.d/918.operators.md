Memory search now recalls what was actually said, not only the facts extracted from it: after each saved chat exchange the engine splits the session into topical segments in the background, and `search_memory` returns the matching ones beside facts, each naming its conversation and turn range. A conversation is readable only by its owner (the member whose chat it is, or the operator for the main session) whatever the data-scoping setting, a workspace conversation reaches only its members, and a conversation with no known owner is recalled by no one; long messages are split, never cut. Segments follow the chat they copy: a deleted session takes its segments with it, and the nightly chat TTL prunes them after 90 days. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#conversation-segments).
