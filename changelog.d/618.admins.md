Tasks that require a device or issuer verification can now preserve an encrypted handoff and offer a status-check button on Personal automation. Acknowledgment and expiry do not complete the task, release its budget or permit another submission; only website confirmation resolves it. Recovery also blocks script-initiated POST requests and WebSockets so a status page cannot repeat checkout through those channels. See [Autonomous execution](AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION.md) for supported handoffs and provider limitations.
