A triage report's executive summary no longer counts against the items it
mentions. "**2 Critical** issues … **2 High** … **3 Low** items" is a tally of
categories, not a verdict on anything, and a summary bullet that then names
one item no longer files that item under every severity the summary counted —
before this, an item filed exactly once under Critical was reported as "under
three verdicts" and, at `enforce`, your agent was re-asked to fix a report that
was already right. Section headings (`## Critical Issues (3)`) and labels
(`**Severity: High**`) work exactly as before. See
[Agents](enterprise/04-agents.md).
