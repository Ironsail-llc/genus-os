A root admin who switches into a child tenant, and that tenant's own admins, can now run the tenant from the Helm: its agents, automations, runs, approvals, channel pairings, memory, accounts, provider keys and audit log. Appliance-wide pages (Controls, Settings, Logs, Plugins, the doctor and marketplace installs) stay with a root admin at home. The audit log now records each event's tenant, so an audit export holds only the tenants you may see; see [Deployment](deployment.md) for the audit routes.
