A tool call over an agent's per-minute limit now waits until the minute has room instead of failing, so a burst of reads no longer reaches the model as "rate limit exceeded"; the run's own deadline bounds the wait. The default limit rises from 30 to 60 calls a minute (`rate_limit_per_minute` still overrides it), and the prompt states the limit the agent is actually held to. Two behavioural rules change: a refusal a person can unlock is explained in plain words with who can grant it, never with throttles, gate names or field names, and a new rule tells an agent in a conversation to lead with the answer, look up what the platform holds rather than ask, and say how old a record is.
