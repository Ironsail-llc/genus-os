Nightly memory maintenance now works on one tenant at a time. Consolidation, insight discovery and relation inference never mix two tenants' facts, and what they produce is stored under the tenant it came from rather than a default one. With row-level security on, child tenants are now maintained as well, and the `get_knowledge_gaps` engine tool answers only for the calling run's tenant. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#lifecycle) for each step.
