Hook workflow runs that the tool-call rate limit defers are now counted per workflow, and the engine logs one summary line per workflow per minute (`workflow=<id> deferred=<n> window=<start>`) instead of one line per run; deferred events are requeued exactly as before. A new doctor check, `deferred_runs.recent`, warns when more than `ROBOTHOR_DEFERRED_RUNS_WARN_THRESHOLD` runs (default 100) were deferred in the last hour, names the workflows that deferred most, and sends a warning alert, which becomes a row in the alert digest and does not page. Past `ROBOTHOR_DEFERRED_RUNS_PAGE_THRESHOLD` (default 500) it sends a critical alert instead, which from the doctor's own process reaches a person only when an alert channel can deliver outside the engine daemon; otherwise it is recorded as an undelivered alert (an `alert_fallback` notification row, shown under the operator agent's unread alerts) and the failing check, whose detail says the page was not delivered, is the signal. Each tier makes one attempt an hour, and an undelivered one is tried again after ten minutes, not on every run; see [Deployment](deployment.md#what-it-checks).
