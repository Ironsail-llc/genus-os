Hook workflow runs that the tool-call rate limit defers are now counted per workflow, and the engine logs one summary line per workflow per minute (`workflow=<id> deferred=<n> window=<start>`) instead of one line per run. A new doctor check, `deferred_runs.recent`, warns when more than `ROBOTHOR_DEFERRED_RUNS_WARN_THRESHOLD` runs (default 100) were deferred in the last hour, names the workflows that deferred most, and sends the warning to the alert channel at most once an hour. Deferred events are requeued exactly as before. See [Deployment](deployment.md#what-it-checks).
