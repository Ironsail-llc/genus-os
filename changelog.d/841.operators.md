`read_file`, `exec` and `execute_code` now refuse credential files under the `credential_path` gate, for every caller including the owner: cloud and Workspace CLI credential directories under `~/.config`, `~/.aws`, `~/.ssh`, `~/.kube`, `~/.docker`, `~/.netrc`, `~/.npmrc`, `~/.pypirc`, `.env` and `.env.*` files, `credentials*.json`, `token*.json`, key files under the home directory and the instance's `.vault-key`. A command is refused when any argument or redirection names such a path, when a glob or symlink resolves onto one, or when a pipeline feeds a directory listing to a reader (`ls ~/.ssh | xargs cat`), on the host and inside a container alike; `ls`, `test`, `stat`, `wc` and `du` on their own are still allowed. `read_file` resolves symlinks before it decides. Hand credentials to agents through the vault, never as files they read. See [Tools](TOOLS.md) for the refusal and the full list.
