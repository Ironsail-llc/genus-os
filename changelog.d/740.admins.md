Inviting someone from Settings › Users & roles now defaults to sign-in through your identity provider when the instance has one, and the copied instructions send the invitee to the dashboard to choose "Sign in with SSO". Before this, an unticked invite made an account that could never sign in, and the instructions pointed at the provider's address, which shows a page of metadata. An SSO invite now stays valid for seven days instead of one hour, and a failed sign-in says the account may not be set up for SSO yet and to ask your administrator. See [Configuration](configuration.md) for how invites and grants work.
