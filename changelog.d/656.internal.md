The mail and calendar [Tools](TOOLS.md) now reach the mailbox through a
workspace provider chosen by `workspace_provider`. Google stays the default and
issues exactly the same `gws` calls as before; the characterization goldens are
byte-identical. Every guard (do-not-contact, no-auto scheduling, dedup,
duplicate-reply, CRM write-through, benchmark refusal) stays in the tool
handlers and runs before any provider call. Selecting `microsoft365` makes the
mail and calendar tools refuse until its providers ship.
