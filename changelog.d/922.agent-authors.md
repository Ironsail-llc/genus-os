`store_memory` now stores through the same write path as every other memory:
a repeated fact reinforces the known one, an update supersedes the older fact,
and a contradiction of an operator-confirmed fact is kept on record while the
operator's fact stays active. Every stored fact also records the people,
organisations and projects it mentions and the relations it states, each tied
to that fact and to who said it (agent-generated for what agents store, external
for emails and web pages). The result adds counts such as `facts_kept_confirmed`
and says `extraction_failed` when the content was kept as written; see
[Agent tools](TOOLS.md#storing-a-memory-store_memory) and the
[Memory System](memory-system.md#write-path).
