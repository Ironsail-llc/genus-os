The engine's flag manifest (`infra/flags.yaml`) carried `ROBOTHOR_RIP_13_MODE`
at `observe`, even though the entry's own soak note records a decision to turn
it off, so `test_manifest_entries_have_required_fields` failed on an
observe-mode flag with neither a planned promotion nor a promotion exemption.
The entry is now `off`, and the systemd drop-in mirror
(`infra/systemd/robothor-engine.service.d/upgrade-rip-flags.conf`) moves with
it. Nothing user-facing changes: neither `observe` nor `off` activates the
feature. One other entry, `ROBOTHOR_BENCHMARK_DECONTAMINATION_MODE`, is an
explicit deferral with no promotion date and still needs an operator decision,
so that test remains red.
