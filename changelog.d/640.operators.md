Pull-request reviews now read what a human reviewer reads: the PR description (runbooks and commands in it included), earlier comments, whether the branch conflicts with its base, and the checks on the head commit — a conflict or a failing check blocks the review. Pull requests from `ROBOTHOR_PR_REVIEW_DEEP_LINES` changed lines (1,500) get a deeper effort, explicit lens passes and a completeness pass, and anything that can pay twice, lose data, leak across tenants or break the base is graded at least major. Reviews run on `opus`, the alias for the newest Opus, and every review footer names the model and effort that wrote it. See [PR reviewer](PR_REVIEWER.md).
