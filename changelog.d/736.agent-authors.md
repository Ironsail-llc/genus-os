Agents can now write to Jira: `jira_create_issue`, `jira_add_comment`,
`jira_transition_issue` and `jira_update_issue` create, comment on, move and
edit tickets as the instance's Jira account. They are opt-in, so name them in
`tools_allowed` or `tools_opt_in` to give them to an agent, and they are denied
to the `user` and `member` roles. See [Tools](TOOLS.md) for the key formats
and how a timed-out create should be checked before a retry.
