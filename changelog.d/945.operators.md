What people tell the assistant about themselves in Telegram and webchat (preferences, purchases, plans, changes in their situation) and the assistant's recommendations are now remembered as facts. Each fact is dated from when it was said, linked to the turns it came from, and attributed to a person only when the turn records that person as its sender. From this release, scheduled runs, sub-agents and workspace runs stop recalling facts learned in one-to-one chats, and existing shared conversation facts stay shared until you run `python -m robothor.memory.conversation_backfill --tenant <id>`, which reports first and changes nothing without `--apply`. `python -m robothor.memory.conversation_skips --tenant <id>` lists and retries any conversation turns that could not be extracted; see [Memory system](memory-system.md#conversation-facts).
