The assistant now organizes the meetings it books: a meeting with guests goes on its own calendar with the operator invited, so it owns the notes and transcript, while events without guests stay on the operator's calendar. On Google Workspace it also turns on Meet transcription and smart notes for those meetings, which needs the `meetings.space.settings` OAuth scope on the assistant's credential; without it the booking still succeeds and the result says transcription was not enabled. See [Tools](TOOLS.md) for the `calendar="operator"` override and the `ROBOTHOR_CALENDAR_MEET_ARTIFACTS` flag.
