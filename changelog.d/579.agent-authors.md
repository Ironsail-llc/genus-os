The Gmail tools return what their descriptions promise: `gws_gmail_search`
gives you sender, subject, date and labels per hit instead of bare ids,
`gws_gmail_get` gives you a decoded `body_text` with attachments listed rather
than inlined, a failed call carries a `hint` instead of a bare exit code, and
every `gws_*` tool is refused on a benchmark run. The calendar tools take a
`calendar` parameter — `operator` (the default) or `own` — because `primary` is
the **assistant's** calendar, and their results carry `calendar`,
`invitations_requested` and `htmlLink` so you can report what actually happened.
`tool_search` now understands the words people use ("check my email", "schedule
a meeting"), answers on a non-deferred run with the agent's own tools instead
of an error, and says outright when a capability does not exist here. The new
[Tools](TOOLS.md) page covers the catalogue, which mail tool is which, whose
calendar is whose, and how deferred loading reaches a tool you cannot see.
