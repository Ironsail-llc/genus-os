Personal automation now distinguishes an accepted checkout from recorded authorization, charges and refunds using an encrypted payment journal. Owners and the assigned agent can read payment status without exposing provider references or changing financial evidence. Duplicate evidence is idempotent, inconsistent evidence remains unresolved, and refunds do not automatically free spending capacity or cancel memberships.
