The CRM now holds an organization structure: a company can sit under a parent
company and carry its ids in outside systems, and a person can hold a role at
a company. A plugin syncing an organization chart writes it with
`upsert_company_by_identifier`, `upsert_person_by_identifier` and
`set_affiliation` from `robothor.crm.dal`, keyed by the outside id, never by
name; repeats change nothing and a deleted record is reported, not revived.
See [Plugins](PLUGINS.md) for building a plugin on the core DAL.
