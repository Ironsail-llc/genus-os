Conversations are now also kept as recallable segments: after each saved chat exchange, the engine splits the session into topical segments in the background and indexes them for vector and keyword search, so a detail no extracted fact kept can still be found. The new `memory_segments` table follows the chat it copies: a deleted session takes its segments with it, and the nightly chat TTL prunes segments older than 90 days. Indexing never delays or fails a turn. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#conversation-segments) for the splitting rule and the retention.
