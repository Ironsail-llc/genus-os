You can now switch a plugin off without uninstalling it, and find out which of your plugins stopped loading and why. The verbs are `genus plugin list`, `info`, `enable`, `disable`, `sync` and `doctor`, and a plugin whose files have changed since you recorded it is held back until you look, rather than loaded quietly. A damaged record never stops the instance booting — but nothing is governing while it is damaged, and the doctor and the Helm both say that plainly instead of implying otherwise. See [Plugins](PLUGINS.md).
