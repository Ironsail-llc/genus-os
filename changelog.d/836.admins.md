Telegram can now show the answer as it is written. With `ROBOTHOR_CHANNEL_STREAMING` on, the "Thinking..." message is edited in place about once a second, shows the tool that is running, and becomes the formatted answer; a transient stream failure is retried once on the same model instead of falling back. Plugin channels can opt in through the inbound pipeline's `stream` callback. See [Configuration](reference/configuration.md).
