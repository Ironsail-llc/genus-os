Several plugins can now extend the same agent. A patch's `append:` adds single
items to a list — a hook identified by its stream and event type, or a value in
`bootstrap_files`, `tools_allowed` and similar — and `detach:` (or removing the
plugin) takes back only those items, so two plugins no longer collide over
`hooks`. Plugins can also ship their own webhook channels as
`docs/webhooks.d/<name>.yaml` and instruction fragments under
`brain/agents/<agent>.d/`. See [Plugins](PLUGINS.md#composable-patches-append-and-detach).
