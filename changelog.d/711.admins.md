A webhook channel can now verify senders that sign Stripe-style
(`t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex>` over the timestamp and the raw body), read its
signature from a header you name, and drop redelivered events by delivery id.
Signature checks on existing channels are unchanged, but on every channel an
event that cannot be written to the bus now answers `503` instead of `200`, so
the sender retries. See [Event Bus](event-bus.md#webhook-ingress) for the
fields.
