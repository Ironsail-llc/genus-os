Database checkouts are cheaper: a connection is checked out without starting a
thread, and the tenant binding and vector-scan setting are sent only when they
change, so a repeat single-statement read costs one round trip instead of five.
The engine and bridge need session-preserving connections to PostgreSQL (direct,
or a session-mode pooler), and the new `db.session_settings` doctor check warns
when a transaction-mode pooler is in the way; see [Deployment](deployment.md#connection-poolers).
