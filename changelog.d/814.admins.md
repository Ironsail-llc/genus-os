The Helm no longer goes blank when one part of it fails to draw: the chat panel and each view show a notice with Retry and Report in their own place while the rest keeps working, and a broken or unknown page gets a proper error or not-found page. List views show placeholder rows while they load, pinch-zoom works on phones, and the theme follows your system's light or dark setting until you pick one with the toggle.
