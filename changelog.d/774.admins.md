The Tasks board in the Helm now fits its columns to the space it actually has
beside the sidebar and the chat panel: four columns when they fit, otherwise
two or one, so cards no longer shrink to slivers or push the page sideways. A
card's title has its own line and its badges wrap on a row beneath it, and the
counters above the board follow the same rule. See
[Web chat (the Helm)](channels/webchat.md).
