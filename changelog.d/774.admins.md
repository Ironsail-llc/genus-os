The Tasks board in the Helm now picks its columns from the space it actually
has beside the sidebar and the chat panel: four when they fit, otherwise two,
otherwise one, so cards no longer shrink to slivers or push the page sideways.
A card's title has its own line, long words wrap inside the card, and badges
sit on a row beneath. The counters above the Tasks and Agents views show two
or four per row the same way. See [Web chat (the Helm)](channels/webchat.md).
