The dashboard image now ships Next.js 16.4.1 and DOMPurify 3.4.17, which close two unauthenticated remote-code-execution advisories in Next.js 16.3.8 and a sanitiser bypass in DOMPurify 3.4.16. No operator action is needed beyond taking the release; see [Deployment](deployment.md) for the upgrade sequence.
