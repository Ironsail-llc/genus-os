You can now measure how accurately conversation ingest extracts facts with
`robothor memory-eval --extraction --extraction-model <local model>`, with no
database needed. It runs the real extraction on labelled generic conversations
and reports precision, recall, how often trust labels, speakers, source turns
and dates are right, and any third-party content stored as the operator's own
words. With `--baseline` it compares the run against the recorded numbers and
exits 2 on a regression. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#conversation-facts)
for what each number means.
