On Microsoft 365, new mail in the assistant's inbox now starts the email pipeline, and changes to the owner's calendar start the calendar pipeline, just as they do on Google. As on Google, each new email is logged once to the CRM as an interaction with its sender, and `triage-inbox.json`, which the email classifier and calendar monitor read, is rebuilt after new mail and every five minutes (`ROBOTHOR_TRIAGE_INBOX_PATH` moves it). A dropped Exchange sync never makes the assistant answer old mail again, and `genus doctor` warns when ingestion falls behind. See [Microsoft 365](workspace/microsoft365.md).
