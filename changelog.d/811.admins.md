A new setting, `ROBOTHOR_WARMUP_PARALLEL` (off by default), makes a chat
turn's warmup cheaper without changing the text the agent sees: memory blocks
are read in one query, entity lookups are batched, and the caller's identity
details are reused across a conversation for up to 15 minutes. Where the
database is slow enough to matter, the warmup sections are also built
concurrently and joined in the same order, and on a fast database they stay
sequential. Each section's time appears on the run as its own warmup step; see
[Configuration reference](reference/configuration.md).
