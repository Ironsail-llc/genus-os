Spend budgets no longer stop agents: when a run reaches the fleet hourly cost cap (`ROBOTHOR_HOURLY_COST_CAP_USD`) or the runaway-token hard cap (`ROBOTHOR_RUNAWAY_HARD_CAP_TOKENS`), the engine records a `budget_stop` step with its reason, raises the existing alert and lets the run continue. The two numbers are now thresholds for that report, and no setting turns either into a stop. Every budget stop, and your own durable stop, is recorded as a step and a telemetry event, and `genus doctor --only budget_stops.recent` counts the last day's stops. See [Configuration](reference/configuration.md).
