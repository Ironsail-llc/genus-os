`view_image` now uses the same fallback ladder `analyze_image` does. When your
agent's own model cannot accept images it gets a description from the local
vision model, or from `ROBOTHOR_VISION_REMOTE_MODEL` when there is no local
one — with `backend`, `model`, `tokens` and `cost_usd` saying who looked and
what it cost, instead of an error. It also refuses the same files its sibling
refuses (outside the workspace, or a credentials file), both tools' answers
carry `provenance` saying the answer came from the image content and not the
filename, and a new behavioural rule 19 tells every agent that an observation
of an item outranks what that item is called. See [Tools](TOOLS.md) for the
rungs and the fields.
