`view_image` now uses the same fallback ladder `analyze_image` does. When your
agent's own model cannot accept images it gets a description from the local
vision model, or from `ROBOTHOR_VISION_REMOTE_MODEL` when there is no local
one — with `backend`, `model`, `tokens` and `cost_usd` saying who looked and
what it cost, instead of an error. Both tools' results now carry `provenance`
and a `provenance_note` saying the answer came from the image content and not
from the filename, and a new behavioural rule 19 tells every agent that an
observation outranks a name. See [Tools](TOOLS.md) for the rungs and the
fields.
