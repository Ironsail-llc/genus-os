The engine, bridge and orchestrator now take `extraVolumes` and `extraVolumeMounts`, lists of raw Kubernetes volume and volumeMount objects that the chart adds to that component's pod as written. Use them to mount something the chart does not model itself, such as a plugin's state directory on its own PersistentVolumeClaim, without forking the chart. Both default to empty and change nothing until set. See [Deployment](deployment.md#mounting-an-extra-volume) for an example and the access-mode caveat for the multi-replica services.
