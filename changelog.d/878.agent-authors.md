Agents can now record and read people's roles at companies and the company
hierarchy: `set_affiliation`, `list_affiliations`, `list_company_children`,
`find_company_by_identifier` and `find_person_by_identifier`, and
`get_contact_360` returns a person's affiliations or, given `companyId`, a
company's parent, children and people by role. Add the names to an agent's
`tools_allowed` to grant them. See [Tools](TOOLS.md) for when to pick each one.
