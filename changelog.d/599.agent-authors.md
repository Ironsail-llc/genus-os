`execute_code` now holds the HTTP your snippet makes on its own — through
`urllib`, `requests` or `http.client` — to the same rule as a `genus_tools`
call: a POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE whose response body your snippet never
printed is counted in `unread_responses`, named as `POST http://host/path` in
`unread_response_tools`, and listed with its status under `http_calls`. A
rejected write (4xx/5xx) and a reply with nothing substantial in it are never
counted. The act→observe reminder now arrives as its own `[SYSTEM]` message,
and under `ROBOTHOR_ACT_OBSERVE_MODE=enforce` a run that tries to finish
without re-reading a source it changed gets one more turn to look, whether or
not it was reminded earlier. See [Agents](enterprise/04-agents.md) for how the
engine's guardrails reach an agent.
