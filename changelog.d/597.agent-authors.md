A triage report that identifies each item in a field rather than in its heading
is now read the way it is written: the block that says `**Message ID:** …` is
the one that decides that item, and the other ids in it — the ones a summary
paragraph mentions in passing — are references, not second verdicts. Before
this, an item decided High in its own section could be reported as "under two
verdicts" because a later summary item mentioned it, and at `enforce` your agent
was re-asked to fix a report that was already right. See
[Agents](enterprise/04-agents.md).
