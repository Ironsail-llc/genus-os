A triage report is now read the way it is written, in three more places: the
block that says `**Message ID:** …` is the one that decides that item, a hedge
or a hand-back belongs to the bullet it is written in rather than to every item
in its section, and a verdict word inside a quoted title (`### 9. "P0 platform
outage" …`) is the item's name rather than its severity. Before this, the two
live runs of one triage task produced nine findings between them and seven were
invented — an item decided High reported as "under two verdicts" because a
later summary mentioned it, and one recap sentence about one item reported
against five others. At `enforce` each of those was a re-ask asking your agent
to fix a report that was already right. See [Agents](enterprise/04-agents.md).
