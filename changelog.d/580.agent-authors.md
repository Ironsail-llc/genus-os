When a task names an output path, a header, a field set or section headings,
the engine now reads those as the contract and checks the workspace against
them before a run may call itself done — not just that the file exists — and it
keeps them in front of your agent: compaction may no longer summarise the
statement of the task away (`ROBOTHOR_COMPACTION_PROTECT_FIRST_N`), and the
output contract is re-stated verbatim after every compaction. The progress
check-in now asks your agent to quote the task's required format and show its
file matches it, a tool that times out says what it actually allows instead of
"skip this step", and `exec` output that was cut says how much was cut and what
to do about it. The settings are listed in the
[Configuration reference](reference/configuration.md).
