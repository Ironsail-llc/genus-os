The chat panel docked beside a Helm view now has a collapse button in its own
header, and a collapsed panel leaves a slim tab at the right edge to bring it
back. The Helm remembers whether you left it open or collapsed, per browser,
so a reload no longer reopens it. See
[Web chat (the Helm)](channels/webchat.md#the-docked-chat-panel).

The Tasks board in the Helm now picks its columns from the space it actually
has beside the sidebar and the chat panel: four when they fit, otherwise two,
otherwise one, so cards no longer shrink to slivers or push the page sideways.
A card's title has its own line, long words wrap inside the card, and badges
sit on a row beneath. The counters above the Tasks and Agents views show two
or four per row the same way. See [Web chat (the Helm)](channels/webchat.md).

The Helm's chat home now opens on a landing before the first message: a greeting with your first name, the Dashboard's figures (services healthy, active tasks, agents online), its quick actions and the suggested prompts, with the composer below. The landing gives way to the conversation as soon as a message arrives, and the chat column is centred at a readable width on wide screens. The docked chat beside other views keeps its compact start, and the full Dashboard is still one click away; [Web chat](channels/webchat.md) covers the chat itself.

Agent replies in the dashboard chat now render as readable Markdown: paragraphs keep their spacing, numbered and bulleted lists show their markers, headings have their sizes, and the text follows the light or dark theme. Links in a reply open in a new tab. Dashboard chat turns also ask the agent to answer first and keep replies short, using headings and lists only where they help. See [Configuration](configuration.md#how-replies-look-in-the-chat-panel).
