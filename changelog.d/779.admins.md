The chat panel docked beside a Helm view now has a collapse button in its own header, a slim tab at the right edge brings it back, and the Helm remembers per browser whether you left it open ([Web chat (the Helm)](channels/webchat.md#the-docked-chat-panel)). The Tasks board and the counters above the Tasks and Agents views pick their columns from the space they actually have beside the sidebar and the chat panel (four, two or one), so cards no longer shrink to slivers or push the page sideways. The chat home opens on a landing before the first message, with a greeting, the Dashboard's figures, its quick actions and the suggested prompts, and gives way to the conversation, centred at a readable width, as soon as a message arrives. Agent replies in the dashboard chat render as readable Markdown that follows the light or dark theme, and dashboard chat turns ask the agent to answer first and keep replies short ([Configuration](configuration.md#how-replies-look-in-the-chat-panel)).
