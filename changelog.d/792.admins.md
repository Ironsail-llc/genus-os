Observe › Logs no longer looks broken on a deployment without journald. It says the deployment writes its logs to standard output and that they are read with your cluster's log tool, and it shows an **Open logs** link when one is configured. Auto-refresh is hidden in that state, and **Refresh** asks again whether journald is available instead of showing a red error. See [Deployment](deployment.md#the-helms-observe-pages).
