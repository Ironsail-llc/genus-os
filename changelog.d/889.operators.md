A Claude Code coding job can no longer read the engine's mounted credentials: its sandbox and file-tool rules now deny `/etc/genus` (where the Helm chart mounts the Google Workspace credential and the vault key), the files and directories `GOOGLE_WORKSPACE_CLI_CREDENTIALS_FILE` and `GOOGLE_WORKSPACE_CLI_CONFIG_DIR` name, the vault key, fingerprint salt and secrets env files wherever their settings put them, and the workspace's own `.vault-key` and `.fingerprint-salt`. Logs and tool results now also redact Google OAuth refresh tokens, `GOCSPX-` client secrets and the `refresh_token` / `client_secret` fields of a credentials file. See [Tools](TOOLS.md).
