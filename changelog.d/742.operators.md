The assistant can now open files attached to email, from Gmail or Microsoft 365
alike: `gws_gmail_get` lists an `attachment_id` for each attachment and the new
`gws_gmail_get_attachment` tool downloads one (up to 20 MB) and reads PDF, Word,
Excel, PowerPoint and text files. Downloads are kept under
`<workspace>/inbox/email/` with the same permissions, credentials quarantine
and retention as Telegram attachments. See [Tools](TOOLS.md) for what it returns.
