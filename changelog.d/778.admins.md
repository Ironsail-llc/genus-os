Agent replies in the dashboard chat now render as readable Markdown: paragraphs keep their spacing, numbered and bulleted lists show their markers, headings have their sizes, and the text follows the light or dark theme. Links in a reply open in a new tab. Dashboard chat turns also ask the agent to answer first and keep replies short, using headings and lists only where they help. See [Configuration](configuration.md#how-replies-look-in-the-chat-panel).
