The Plugins page now shows, on each plugin's card, the agents it installed (with their schedules) and the workflows it shipped (with their triggers), and says "none" when it ships neither. The Workflows list under Automations now includes workflows defined in `docs/workflows/` that have never run, each labelled with where it came from: the plugin that shipped it, or `workspace`. See [Plugins](PLUGINS.md#seeing-what-a-plugin-shipped).
