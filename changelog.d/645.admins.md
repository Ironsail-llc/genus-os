Agents can now edit existing meetings directly: `gws_calendar_update` reschedules, renames, edits notes or location, and adds or removes guests in one call, keeping existing guests and their RSVPs. `gws_calendar_respond` accepts, declines or tentatively accepts an invitation. `gws_calendar_add_attendees` now writes directly, with no draft and no "reply yes to confirm" step. The `ROBOTHOR_CALENDAR_OPERATIONS_ENABLED` setting is removed: grant the new tools in your agent's manifest, and remove any drop-in that set that variable. See [Tools](TOOLS.md#whose-calendar).
