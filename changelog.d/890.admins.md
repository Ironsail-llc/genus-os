The RIP-13 (symbolic short-term memory) flag is now turned off the way the
drop-in header documents — `ROBOTHOR_RIP_13_ENABLED=0` with no `MODE` line —
instead of `MODE=off` alongside `ENABLED=1`. `ROBOTHOR_RIP_13_MODE` accepts
only `observe`/`enforce`, so a `MODE=off` line clamps back to `observe` and
never actually disabled the flag. That clamp is why `flag_audit.py` kept
reporting RIP-13 as drifted against the manifest's recorded `off`; see
[Configuration](reference/configuration.md).
