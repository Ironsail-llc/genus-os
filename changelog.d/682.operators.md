The cache and event bus is now Valkey, deployed per instance by the chart's own
subchart rather than Redis. On upgrade, rename `redis.enabled` to
`valkey.enabled` in your values and move any `redis.*` server overrides (image,
storage, resources) under `valkey.*`; `redis.host` and `redis.port` keep their
meaning for pointing at a cache the chart does not deploy. `global.imagePullSecrets`
is now a list of secret names rather than `{name: ...}` entries. See
[Deployment](deployment.md) for the chart's toggles.
