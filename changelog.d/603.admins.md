You can now set short-term goals that keep working until achieved, and long-term goals that pursue tasks and wait for dates or events. Every goal carries four ceilings — a token budget, a cost ceiling, a maximum number of runs and a deadline — so a goal that keeps reporting progress still stops and asks you before spending more; each is overridable when you set the goal and raisable when you resume it. Open Goals to enable execution for a tenant, inspect evidence, progress and remaining budget, or pause and resume work. A CRM task linked to a goal waits only while that goal is paused, blocked or awaiting review — finishing the goal, or switching goal execution off, hands every linked task straight back to its agent's inbox — see [Goal pursuit](features/goal-pursuit.md).
