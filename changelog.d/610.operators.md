A confirmed meeting attendee draft now executes without another model call, keeping existing guests and RSVPs and stopping after one verification; the confirmation binds only to your own still-open draft, is used once, and is skipped in plan mode, so a plain "yes" can never replay a finished operation. Every calendar result names the calendar it applied to, reports a declined guest as declined, and says whether the meeting recurs. An interrupted write that provably never reached the calendar now clears its own reconciliation hold instead of freezing that meeting, and interactive runs report progress and no longer repeat ineffective context compaction. Turning `ROBOTHOR_CALENDAR_OPERATIONS_ENABLED` on or off needs an engine restart; see [Interactive request performance](runbooks/INTERACTIVE_REQUEST_PERFORMANCE.md) for rollout and for clearing a hold by hand.
