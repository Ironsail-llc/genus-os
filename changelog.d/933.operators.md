The Datadog watch now also notices a tenant's unmuted Datadog monitors that
start firing (Alert, Warn or No Data) or recover, and a service that was
logging at least once a minute and has gone quiet, recording again when it
logs. Like the error-spike findings, these are `datadog.watch_finding` audit
events marked `dry_run`, and nothing is sent. See
[Observability](OBSERVABILITY.md#genus-watches-datadog).
