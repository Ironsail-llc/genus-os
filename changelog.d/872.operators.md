The `deferred_runs.recent` doctor check now reports what is waiting in each hook workflow's backlog, its depth and the age of its oldest event, and fails with a warning alert when any backlog's oldest event has waited more than an hour. The engine's per-window deferral line ends with `backlog=<depth> oldest=<age>s`. A run cut off by an engine stop is started again at the next start. See [Deployment](deployment.md#what-it-checks).
