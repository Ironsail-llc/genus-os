Failures that used to be swallowed without a trace now leave a log line, and
a failing outage detector, autoDream staleness check or user-cron tick logs at
WARNING instead of DEBUG, so a broken alarm is visible in the journal. The
orchestrator's `GET /health` now reports `components.vision` (`available`,
`last_error`) for the vision proxy without changing the overall status. See
[Deployment](deployment.md) for the health endpoints.
