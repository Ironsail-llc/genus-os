Events an agent creates for you now land on **your** calendar with an invitation, instead of on the assistant's own Google calendar with you as an attendee and no mail sent — this needs your calendar shared with the instance's account with "Make changes to events", which `genus doctor --category calendar` now checks and names the fix for. `genus doctor` also gains three advisory checks — instructions naming a tool the manifest does not grant, a manifest that denies a Google tool while its `exec_allowlist` admits the CLI doing the same job, and an agent that can delete a CRM person, company, note or task with no human in the loop (see [Tools](TOOLS.md)). The Helm chart now arms the human-approval gate at `observe`, which records those escalations without blocking anything. Promoting it to `enforce`, which actually denies a delete nobody approved, stays your decision once an approver is wired and soaked — `docs/runbooks/approval-enforce.md` has the steps, and until then `genus doctor` reporting the gate unarmed is the expected state rather than a fault.
