The Helm chart has an optional `hooks` component: the webhook receiver in its
own pods (two replicas, rolling updates, a disruption budget), so webhook
deliveries keep landing while the engine restarts. Turn it on with
`hooks.enabled: true` and list your channels under `hooks.channels`; it is off
by default and nothing changes until you do. See
[Webhook receiver (hooks)](deployment.md#webhook-receiver-hooks).
