You can now retire a memory fact, export the audit log as CSV, and read a unit's journal over the API, none of it needing a shell on the box. A forget shows you what it would deactivate before you commit it with a reason, and the audit export admits the read-only `auditor` role, so that job can go to someone who is not given the rest of the instance. See [Deployment](deployment.md) for the routes and what each one is gated on.
