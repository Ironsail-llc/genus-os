The Logs page now has Traces, Monitors and Activity tabs beside the Datadog log search: error traces open as a tree of their spans, monitors show firing ones first, and Activity lists the Datadog alerts and watch findings Genus recorded, each marked recorded, not sent. A log line with a trace id links to its trace, and a run's drawer in Runs links to that run's log lines. The root organization's owner or admin, at home, also sees every organization's errors and firing monitors at the top of the page. See [the Logs page](OBSERVABILITY.md#for-people-the-logs-page).
