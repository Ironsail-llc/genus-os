When the Channels page cannot list the channels, it now says why: the bridge
could not reach the engine, the engine refused the bridge's request, the
engine does not serve the listing (a version mismatch), or which status the
engine answered. The cards from the last successful read stay on screen,
marked as possibly out of date. See [Channel access](channels/access.md).

When the Plugins page cannot read the plugin set, it now says which hop failed:
the bridge could not reach the engine at its configured URL, or the dashboard
could not reach the bridge. A refresh that fails after a good read keeps the
last listing on screen, marked as unconfirmed, with its switches locked until a
read succeeds. See [Plugins](PLUGINS.md).
