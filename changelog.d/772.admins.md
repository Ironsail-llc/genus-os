When the Channels page cannot list the channels, it now says why (the bridge
could not reach the engine, the engine refused the request, a version mismatch,
or the status the engine answered) and keeps the cards from the last good read,
marked as possibly out of date. When the Plugins page cannot read the plugin
set, it says which hop failed, the bridge-to-engine link or the
dashboard-to-bridge link, and a refresh that fails after a good read keeps the
last listing on screen, marked as unconfirmed, with its switches locked. See
[Channel access](channels/access.md) and [Plugins](PLUGINS.md).
