Inviting someone from Settings › Users & roles now defaults to sign-in through the
organisation's identity provider whenever the instance has one. The copied sign-in
instructions tell the invitee to open the dashboard and choose "Sign in with SSO". Before
this, an invite left unticked created an account that could never sign in through SSO,
and the instructions pointed at the identity provider's address, which shows a page of
metadata instead of a sign-in screen.
