The owner or admin of a parent tenant now has a Command centre in the Helm: one card per company, showing how its agents, schedules and tasks are doing, with the companies that need attention first. A company that could not be read says so instead of showing zeros, and clicking a card switches into that company when tenant switching is on. It is hidden for everyone else, and each look at a child company is recorded in that company's audit log. See [Deployment](deployment.md#the-command-centre).
