An agent now sees only the credentials its manifest grants it. Name them under `secrets:` and the environment of every process the agent execs is scrubbed to that grant plus a short allowlist, with `ROBOTHOR_EXEC_ENV_MODE` as the ladder from `off` through `observe` to `enforce`; a sub-agent inherits nothing, since its grants are read from its own manifest. `vault_get` no longer hands a value to the model — it answers with a one-way fingerprint, and the new `vault_test` dials the vendor's identity endpoint so an agent can prove a credential works and say who it authenticates as without ever reading it. Read a credential through `robothor.secrets` rather than the process environment, and see [Configuration](configuration.md) for the grant syntax, the ladder and what the scrub leaves behind.
