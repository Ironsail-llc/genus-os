The engine reaches READY faster after a restart. Startup steps that do not
depend on one another now run concurrently, the manifest directory is read
once, and the database pool and every agent's system prompt are warm before
the first turn. `/health` reports `startup_ms`, and one `STARTUP` log line
names the cost of each step. See [Deployment](deployment.md#health-endpoints).

Chat history restored after a restart keeps each question before its reply;
the two share a timestamp and could come back swapped.
