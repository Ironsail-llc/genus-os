You can now roll a deployment back to the previous release after a newer one has applied migrations. The older release starts beside migrations numbered strictly above everything it ships, warns which ones they are, and `genus doctor` keeps reporting `db.migrations` until you roll forward; a gap, a renamed or drifted migration, or a newer row while one of its own is pending is still refused. This works only for rollbacks to releases that include it. In a child tenant's engine, `identity.owner_account` now passes as not applicable, since a child has no owner by design. See [Deployment](deployment.md#rolling-back-to-an-older-release).
