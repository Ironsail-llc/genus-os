The Helm gains a sales pilot: integration setup that never takes a secret,
bounded research requests, a prospect and contact review console, measured
weekly reports, and a two-step deployment that binds the fleet to one reviewed,
verified release. Role permissions tighten with it — migration 138 denies
`web_render` and the `sales_*` tools to the `service`, `user` and `member`
defaults, so a sales agent now needs its own role rather than inheriting the
catch-all allow, and `admin` and `owner` are unchanged. See
[Native sales intelligence](SALES_INTELLIGENCE.md) and
[Agents](enterprise/04-agents.md).
