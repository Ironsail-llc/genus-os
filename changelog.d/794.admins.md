Settings › Flags now recovers from a session that lapsed mid-visit: it retries once, and if the session has really ended it shows **Sign in** instead of a red error beside controls it cannot apply, and a failed reload clears the flags rather than leaving stale ones on screen. Every flag change now needs a non-blank reason at the Bridge as well as in the page, and appears in the audit log as `control.set` with who made it, the value and the reason.
