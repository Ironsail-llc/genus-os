The `agents.tools_named_but_not_granted` doctor check had two false positives
on a live instance: it reported the three meta-tools (`tool_search`,
`tool_describe`, `tool_call`) as named-but-not-granted for every deferred
agent, because it modelled the registry's strip and not the injection that
puts them back; and it judged `tools_allowed` against the built-in schemas
only, so every MCP-adapter tool an instance grants read as "resolves to
nothing". Both are fixed against the loader's own rules — `should_defer` for
the first, the adapters' declared `tools_allowed` for the second.
