Steer and stop a running task from every chat surface, and see its progress there, with `ROBOTHOR_STEER_EVERYWHERE` on. In the Helm the composer steers the running task while a reply streams and Stop halts that tab's run; Slack gains `/steer` and `/stop`, the Teams channel accepts them typed, and both post one progress line per run that is edited in place. See [Web chat (the Helm)](channels/webchat.md), [Slack](channels/slack.md) and [Microsoft Teams](channels/teams.md).
