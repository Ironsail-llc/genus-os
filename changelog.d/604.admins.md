Open Account → Personal automation to enroll profiles, website logins, documents
and payment resources in the native vault, then grant bounded standing authority.
Authorized agents can submit supported forms without another approval, with
operation tracking, protected frame inspection, validation before filling and
reconciliation for uncertain outcomes. See
[Personal autonomous execution](AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION.md) for setup, browser
requirements and the separate real-payment deployment gate.

Recorded membership renewals count toward monthly budgets, including free-trial
charges in future months, and the dashboard shows saved caps, projected charges
and available profile fields with enrollment provenance.
