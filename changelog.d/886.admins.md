Agents now know which organisation they serve: every interactive turn, and every scheduled run that builds a preamble, names this tenant, its parent, the child tenants the conversation may reach and what it may do in them. An owner or admin who switches into a child tenant keeps their name and role in the conversation. The operator-facing agent also starts a conversation with the fleet roster and each agent's schedule state.
