Agents now know which organisation they serve: every interactive turn, and every scheduled run that builds a preamble, names this tenant, its parent, the child tenants the conversation may reach and what it may do in them. An owner or admin who switches into a child tenant keeps their name and role in the conversation. The operator-facing agent also starts a conversation with the fleet roster and each agent's schedule state. See [Agents, skills and plugins](enterprise/04-agents.md#what-an-agent-knows-about-where-it-is).

In a tenant hierarchy, the parent's agent can now see and act in the child tenants: the parent's owner or admin can ask it how every company is doing, and drill into one. Only a top-level run reaches a child, never a sub-agent or a benchmark run, and only the verified owner can update a child's tasks or notes. A scheduled agent reaches the children only when you set the parent's `child_data_access` and the agent's manifest opts in. See [Tools](TOOLS.md#child-tenants-org_overview-the-tenant-argument-and-dry_run); the tenant hierarchy runbook covers the grants.
