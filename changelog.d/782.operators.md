A root-tenant owner or admin can now switch into a descendant tenant and act there, through `POST /api/auth/switch-tenant` or the Helm's top-bar picker, with the acting tenant carried in the access token, every signed-in request bound to its token's tenant under row-level security, and a child tenant's admin able to reach neither its parent nor a sibling; it stays off until you set `GENUS_TENANT_SWITCH_MODE` (`observe` logs what it would do, `enforce` issues tokens and insists that RLS is genuinely enforcing). One Helm release can now run an engine per child tenant from the chart's new `tenants:` list, each with its own Service, workspace volume and event consumer group (`GENUS_EVENT_CONSUMER_GROUP`), reached by the bridge and dashboard through `GENUS_TENANT_ENGINE_URLS` or `GENUS_TENANT_ENGINE_URL_TEMPLATE`; this requires `vaultKey.enabled` and a `vault-key` Vault path holding a base64 `key` and `fingerprint-salt` shared by the bridge and every engine (copy an existing `.vault-key` and `.fingerprint-salt` rather than generating new ones, or every stored secret becomes unreadable), and the chart refuses to render if `engine.env` pins a tenant other than `global.rootTenant`. A tenant can claim email domains with `genus tenant set-domains`, so new SSO users from those domains are provisioned into it, and `genus tenant move-account --email alice@example.com --to acme-child-a` moves an existing account with every linked row in one transaction, revokes its sessions and audits the move (run it with `--dry-run` first). See [Deployment](deployment.md) for the tenant list and the switcher, [Event Bus](event-bus.md#one-engine-per-tenant) for the consumer groups, [Configuration](configuration.md) for the routes and [Settings](reference/configuration.md) for the new settings.
