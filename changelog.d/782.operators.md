One release can now serve a root tenant and its child tenants: each entry in the chart's new `tenants:` list runs its own engine with its own workspace volume, event consumer group (`GENUS_EVENT_CONSUMER_GROUP`) and plugin directory on that volume, reads its own provider secret class `engine-providers-<id>`, and is reached by the bridge and dashboard through `GENUS_TENANT_ENGINE_URLS` or `GENUS_TENANT_ENGINE_URL_TEMPLATE`, while every signed-in request is bound to its token's tenant under row-level security. Turn on switching with `tenancy.switchMode: enforce` (`GENUS_TENANT_SWITCH_MODE`, which refuses unless RLS is genuinely enforcing): a root-tenant owner or admin then acts in a child from the Helm's top-bar picker, a child's admin reaches neither its parent nor a sibling, and the release needs `vaultKey.enabled` with a `vault-key` Vault path (`key` and `fingerprint-salt`) shared by the bridge and every engine — copy an existing `.vault-key` and `.fingerprint-salt` rather than generating new ones, or every stored secret becomes unreadable. The root tenant decides which plugins a child may run with `PUT /api/plugins/{name}/tenants/{tenant_id}` (or Settings › Plugins): a plugin it forces off is refused by that child's engine, even across a restart while grants cannot be read, and one it forces on stays enabled whatever the child's admins choose. `genus tenant set-domains` routes new SSO users by email domain, and `genus tenant move-account` and `genus tenant move-workload` move an account, or a workload's history of runs, steps, schedules, workflow runs and chat sessions, to another tenant in one audited transaction, from the root's shell only and with `--dry-run` first; see [Deployment](deployment.md#running-child-tenants-in-one-release), [Plugins](PLUGINS.md#per-tenant) and [Settings](reference/configuration.md).
