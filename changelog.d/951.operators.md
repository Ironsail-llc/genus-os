Memory recall no longer loses an answer from a past conversation when many extracted facts name the same subject: when facts fill the reranker's head, the best conversation segment is judged beside them and kept, in the head's last place, when it is relevant. When the next result does not fit the recall context and at least 300 characters remain, it is cut to fit instead of being skipped. The context size is the new setting `ROBOTHOR_MEMORY_RECALL_CONTEXT_CHARS` (default 4,000 characters; restart the engine and bridge after changing it). See [Memory System](memory-system.md) and [Settings](reference/configuration.md).
