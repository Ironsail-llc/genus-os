A plugin channel can now serve many conversations at once: messages in one
conversation run in order, different conversations run side by side, and every
successful exchange is remembered in the conversation's history. `handle_message`
takes `record_turn` and `history_limit` to opt out of that memory or bound it,
and `ROBOTHOR_CHANNEL_MAX_CONCURRENT_RUNS` (default 8) caps the runs all
channels have in flight, queueing rather than refusing. See
[Plugins](PLUGINS.md) and [Configuration](configuration.md).
