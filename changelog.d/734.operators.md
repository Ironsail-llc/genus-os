A Google instance can now ingest the assistant's Gmail on push instead of
polling: a Pub/Sub push subscription posts to a new `google_oidc` webhook
channel, and the engine reads the inbox's Gmail history and publishes
`email.new` once per new message. It is off until `ROBOTHOR_GMAIL_PUSH_ENABLED`
is set, and the engine renews the Gmail watch itself when
`ROBOTHOR_GMAIL_PUSH_TOPIC` names the topic. See
[Event Bus](event-bus.md#gmail-push) for the setup steps.
