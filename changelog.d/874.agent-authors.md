Agents now relay a refusal as the refusal itself states it: one that names its gate is still relayed with the gate and its unlock, and one from another system that names no gate is reported in its own words, with its cause stated as not given and the system's administrator named as who can check. A refusal is final for that action for the rest of the run: the agent stops and offers who can grant it, and never seeks the same effect through another tool, shell or code, another credential or connection, or by reading source, configuration or the environment. A message to an end customer carries no error text, tool names, setting names or ids. See [Tools](TOOLS.md#when-a-tool-is-refused).
