A tenant can now claim email domains with `genus tenant set-domains acme-child-a example.com`, and a new SSO user from a claimed domain is provisioned into that tenant rather than the default one.
`genus tenant move-account --email alice@example.com --to acme-child-a` moves an existing account and every row linked to it in one transaction, revokes its sessions and audits the move; run it with `--dry-run` first.
If the account's CRM contact has history in its current tenant the move is refused with the counts, and `--detach-person` leaves that contact where it is and gives the account a new one.
See the [CLI reference](reference/cli.md) for every flag; the tenant hierarchy runbook in the repository walks through creating a child tenant end to end.
