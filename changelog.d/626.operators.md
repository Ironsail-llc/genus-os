Turning autonomous execution off, or revoking a grant, now stops a task being finished as well as being started. Reconciling an uncertain result, arming an external-verification check and recovering one after a restart all re-read your switch and your grant first, and the feature ships disabled and inert: nothing polls, nothing claims, no browser is kept open. A verification that lapses no longer leaves its budget stuck — the task moves to "waiting for you" and you can clear it from the Personal automation page. See [Autonomous execution](AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION.md) for the states and what each refusal means.

Personal automation is now off unless you turn it on. Set `ROBOTHOR_AUTONOMY_ENABLED=true` to offer it on this instance; until then there is no `/account/autonomy` page, no autonomy paragraph on any agent's system prompt and no autonomy wording in the browser tool's schema. `robothor-autonomy.service` also has a start limit now, so a persistent misconfiguration stops and pages you with the cause instead of restarting every five seconds in silence. See [Autonomous execution](AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION.md) to turn it on.
