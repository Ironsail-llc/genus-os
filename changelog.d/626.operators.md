Personal automation is now off unless you turn it on, and turning it off stops a task being finished as well as being started: reconciling an uncertain result, arming an external-verification check and recovering one after a restart all re-read your switch and your grant first. Until you set `ROBOTHOR_AUTONOMY_ENABLED=true` there is no Personal automation page, no autonomy paragraph on any agent's prompt and no autonomy wording in the browser tool, so an instance that never enrols anything pays nothing for the feature. A verification that lapses no longer leaves its budget stuck — the task moves to "waiting for you" and you can clear it from the Personal automation page. See [Autonomous execution](AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION.md) for the states and what each refusal means.
