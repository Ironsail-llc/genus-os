Personal automation is off unless you turn it on, and turning it off now stops a task being finished as well as being started — reconciling a result, arming a verification check and recovering one after a restart all re-read your switch and your grant first. A verification that lapses no longer leaves its budget stuck: the task moves to "waiting for you" and you can clear it. **Erase submission record** removes the page text a browser observed on your behalf — your name, date of birth, address and the answers you gave — keeping only that a record was taken, when, and under which grant; records also expire on their own after a year. See [Configuration](configuration.md) for the switch and the retention setting.
