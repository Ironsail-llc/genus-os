Genus now watches each tenant's Datadog telemetry on its own: every 5 minutes
it notices a service whose error logs jump well above that service's own
recent level, and the root tenant's engine notices when either Datadog key is
rejected or Datadog cannot be reached. Findings are recorded, not delivered:
each is a `datadog.watch_finding` audit event holding the brief it would send
and to whom. Delivery arrives in a later release. See
[Observability](OBSERVABILITY.md#genus-watches-datadog).
