Agents sorting or labelling a folder of images now see *why* the vision model
answered as it did, and behavioural rule 18 tells them a filename is a claim
about a picture rather than evidence of it. This closes a measured failure where
an agent got 93% of a hundred-image sort right, distrusted its own answers
because the filenames disagreed, and shipped the filenames instead. No setting
changes, and the inline result stays under the same cap as before. See
[Tools](TOOLS.md).
