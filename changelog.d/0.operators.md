Memory now ties each CRM person and company to exactly one entity. A name in a
fact resolves to an entity only on strong evidence: a CRM id, an email or a
channel identifier, or a unique full name with supporting context. A first name
alone is never enough, and every decision is recorded and can be reversed with
`genus memory-identity reverse`. To bring existing facts in, run
`genus memory-identity sync` and then `genus memory-identity backfill --dry-run`
to review what would change; see [Memory System](memory-system.md#identity).
