Memory search now follows the knowledge graph: facts up to two links away from the people and things a question names are recalled and keep a place in the results even when they share no words with the question, each labelled with its origin and the path that reached it. The walk only crosses links the reader is allowed to see, never leaves the tenant, and never runs through the operator's own entry. Automatic recall at the start of a conversation uses only operator-confirmed, user-stated and CRM evidence, within a 150 ms budget. Existing memory has no recorded origin yet, so that step keeps today's name-matched context until new writes record one. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#associative-recall).
