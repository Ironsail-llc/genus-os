Memory search now recalls what was actually said, not only the facts extracted from it: after each saved chat exchange the engine splits the session into topical segments in the background, and `search_memory` returns the matching ones beside facts, each naming its conversation and turn range. A person's own conversation is private to that person whatever the data-scoping setting, and a workspace conversation reaches only its members; long messages are split, never cut. Segments follow the chat they copy: a deleted session takes its segments with it, and the nightly chat TTL prunes them after 90 days. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#conversation-segments).
