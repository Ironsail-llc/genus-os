Memory search now follows the knowledge graph: facts up to two links away from the people and things a question names are recalled and keep a place in the results even when they share no words with the question, each labelled with its origin and the path that reached it. The walk crosses only links the reader may see, never leaves the tenant and never runs through the operator's own entry. Automatic recall at the start of a conversation uses only operator-confirmed, user-stated and CRM evidence within a 150 ms budget, so on existing memory, which has no recorded origin yet, it keeps today's name-matched context. See [Memory System](memory-system.md#associative-recall).
